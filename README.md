One joke about Monday mornings, at most 200 characters, entered in the
[make.fun competition "Monday Morning Punchlines"](https://make.fun/c/u-gentle-badger-68-monday-morning-punchlines-709645dc).

## The joke

> I tried to negotiate with Monday. It offered me Tuesday and called it a promotion.

82 characters, one line. Monday as a bad boss across the negotiating table;
Tuesday — the most ordinary day of the week — reframed as a "promotion".
No alarm, no coffee, no "I hate Mondays": a fresh angle with a real punchline.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes the joke to /output/entry.txt
```

`main.ts` validates the gate itself before writing: 1–200 characters,
not only whitespace, single line.
